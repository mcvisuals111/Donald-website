02 — The first thing we cover

The stop is the only number on the chart you control.

Everything else — where it goes, how fast, whether it comes back — is the market's decision, not yours.

03 — How a trade gets measured

Two times what you're risking, or the trade isn't worth taking.

Not dollars. R — multiples of the amount you had at risk. It's the only way to compare one trade to the next.

07 — Who you're learning from

Donald Mackenzie has spent the last few years trading the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq, mostly through futures.

He's not a fund manager and he doesn't want your money to trade. He sits down with one person at a time and teaches them how the index actually moves — what a candle is telling you, where the risk is, how much to size, and what to do when the trade goes against you.

He's taught before, and the one-on-one format is deliberate. Most trading education is a video course you buy at midnight and never finish. This is an hour, in person, on your own screen, where you can stop him and ask why.
